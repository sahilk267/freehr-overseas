# Versioned offers, candidate responses and joining handoff

Date: 12 November 2026. Scope: M19-F01–F05 with the existing permission, approval, queue, mail, follow-up, commercial and joining services. The complete advanced roadmap remains open.

## Implemented behavior

- Owned placements use one offer identity and immutable versions. Compensation distinguishes annual base, guaranteed allowances, non-guaranteed variable targets and one-time payments. Terms include currency, benefits, role/location, conditions, joining date/timezone and response deadline. Owner-recorded client authority sets current minimum/maximum fixed compensation and expiry; it does not claim independently authenticated client consent.
- Every release requires the existing mandatory owner approval, bound to the exact version, terms hash, source facts, client-authority revision, sender, fee basis and accepted agency contract. Stale requests fail. Private agency fee basis is explicitly selected as annual base, annual fixed or annual target, with evidence; one-time payments never silently enter the annual basis. No parallel contract or invoice engine is introduced.
- Public links expose only masked email, role/version and deadline before email verification. Tokens stay in URL fragments, are removed on entry, and are never persisted in browser storage. Full terms and response actions require a short-lived hashed session established by an owner-authorized email code. Codes are generated at transport time, not stored in queued message text. Links/codes bind current version, recipient, authority, origin and signing-secret generation.
- Code requests are deduplicated, capped at three per recipient/day and spaced by sixty seconds. Codes expire after ten minutes, allow five failed verification attempts and are single-use. Sessions expire after ten minutes. The owner must explicitly authorize the transactional-code policy at release: that scope may bypass ordinary outreach quiet time/recipient spacing, while the workspace outbound budget, managed pause and emergency stop remain enforced. Link emails retain normal review/outbox authorization. Provider acceptance is recorded separately from actual recipient delivery.
- Candidate accept, decline and counter responses record exact version/hash, typed name, notice and email-possession evidence. Conflicting repeats fail; identical retries reuse the receipt. A dedicated `offer_response` consent purpose does not grant marketing, AI processing or unrelated recruitment rights. Email possession is not independently verified legal identity or a qualified electronic signature.
- Acceptance updates the placement compensation through an exact, immutable commercial amendment referencing the original agency binding and offer receipt. Canonical integer fee/tax calculation remains authoritative; invoice history and changed agency terms prevent an unreviewed amendment. Decline/counter does not close employment or make a hiring decision. A verified counter can prepare a fresh draft within current client bounds; a fresh owner release is still required.
- Supersession/closure cancels unused approvals, queued mail and FU21 reminders and revokes old capabilities. Shared-link enrollment uses the existing follow-up engine and stops on response, expiry, source changes or withdrawal. One durable deadline task on the existing managed worker expires unanswered offers for owner review, without deciding employment.
- Versioned acceptance is required before joining transitions. Required conditions need current owner-reviewed evidence; regulated, notice and relocation evidence requires issuer/reference and future validity. A later blocked review overrides earlier completion. These records do not certify visa/work authorization. Candidate withdrawal reports hold joining/checkpoint work for owner review and do not establish legal termination. Accepted terms cannot be silently edited or unilaterally revoked.
- Owner controls provide version history, bounded authority, structured drafts, release, private link, reviewed email, sharing evidence, reminders, counter drafts, condition review and an escaped offer pack. The public page displays exact terms, response and withdrawal controls. Candidate erasure revokes capabilities and redacts offer/session/OTP/compensation evidence and recipient-bearing offer mail approvals.

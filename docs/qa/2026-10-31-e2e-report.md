# FreelanceHR live end-to-end review — 31 October 2026

`https://freelancehr.overseasjob.in` par personal owner login karke actual browser, authenticated APIs aur persistent MySQL workflows test kiye. Google login ki zarurat nahi hai. Main recruitment journey ab prospect se joining, invoice aur synthetic receipt settlement tak chalti hai. Testing mein mile functional defects fix karke GitHub `main` par push kiye aur deployed site par retest kiya.

Neeche configuration dependencies, missing implementation aur tested functionality alag diye hain.

## Actual journeys jo pass hue

1. **Client to approved job:** synthetic prospect create/reload → research/qualification/proposal/conversion → contact and private KYB evidence → owner-recorded verification → fee agreement and recorded acceptance → owner onboarding approval → job intake and actual client-confirmation email → sourcing state.
2. **Candidate to controlled shortlist:** candidate creation and combined search filters → private CV upload/quarantine/scan/access → purpose-specific consent → evidence match → screening evidence and approved positive disposition → governed shortlist sharing state. Shared-state edits/direct sharing bypasses were rejected. Viewed-shortlist feedback was saved and read back separately.
3. **Interview to placement:** schedule in selected timezone → confirm → reschedule and increment ICS sequence → reminder toggle/export → complete interview → structured feedback → owner progression decision → offer/acceptance → joining evidence and approval → invoice eligibility and guarantee tracking.
4. **Invoice to recorded settlement:** invoice draft → owner issue approval → HTML invoice → payment promise/cancellation without fictional funds → partial receipt → repeated-event deduplication → overpayment rejection → final receipt and zero balance. The browser journey used INR 40 + INR 60 against an INR 100 synthetic invoice; rejected INR 70 did not change its INR 60 remaining balance.
5. **Privacy and communication boundaries:** correction request/fulfilment → deletion with redacted tombstones → old document URL denied; withdrawal and DNC prevented fresh communication permission. A mislabeled PDF stayed quarantined. Synthetic inbound threading, STOP suppression, authenticated webhook correlation and replay deduplication worked.
6. **Owner operations:** team invite/role change/revoke, incident lifecycle and audit, operating settings save/restore, emergency stop restore, unchanged-content policy version/activation, queue enqueue/retry/cancel and safe runner invocation. AI scoring work recorded an explicit missing-key failure rather than a fabricated score.

Internal `shared`, agreement acceptance, client confirmation, consent and bank receipt records are owner-recorded evidence. These tests do not prove that a real client received a submission, signed an agreement, granted consent or cleared a payment.

### Dependency audit finding

`pnpm audit --prod --json` reported **151 advisory records against the committed pnpm lock graph: 3 critical, 51 high, 77 moderate and 20 low**. Critical records include `fast-xml-parser`, `tar` and `proxy-addr`; advisory links and patched-version ranges are in the evidence JSON. Build dependencies also sit in the project's dependency list, so this count is not a measure of internet-reachable vulnerabilities.

Hostinger installs with npm, while the repository declares pnpm and has a pnpm lockfile. `npm audit` could not audit the current checkout because there is no npm lockfile. Therefore the pnpm result does not attest to the exact live npm-resolved tree. No exploitability claim or clean-security claim is made, and no automatic breaking `audit fix --force` upgrade was applied during this functional review. The dependency graph and its advisories remain an explicit unresolved release-quality item.

## Test corrections, remaining scope and cleanup

Earlier raw scripts contain failures from stale selectors, reading options before the query completed, expecting 403 instead of the actual 401, assuming an incident response was an array, and expecting erased document rows to disappear instead of retaining redacted tombstones. These assumptions were corrected and the relevant assertions rerun. The evidence records those retests; raw historical failures were not silently counted as passes.

Queue scripts initially refused to process tasks outside their narrow fixture allowlist. Later checks identified the known synthetic timer tasks, verified the missing-key result and cancelled QA tasks. Safe refusal was not a product bug or successful worker outcome.

Five remaining guard-only procedures are explicitly listed in the CSV: three alternative consequential request routes, `team.accept` and `system.notifyOwner`. Successful equivalent joining/invoice/share business actions were verified through the main routes, but that does not count as a positive test of every alternative route. No claim of exhaustive field combinations, every state transition, load behavior or broad accessibility coverage is made.

All created business data carries the `E2E-20261031103153` label or an associated synthetic fixture reference. No real email, recruiter notification, bank transfer or gateway checkout was sent. Both synthetic invoices were settled only in the application's test ledger. QA sender identities were disabled, synthetic memberships revoked, and known QA pending work/future appointments cancelled or completed. Original effective operating settings and policy content were restored. Identical-content policy versions, audit history and labelled recruitment/ledger records remain for review; the database was not wiped.

The active policy version's metadata can therefore differ from its initial version even though its effective configuration is unchanged. Deletion intentionally retains minimal redacted tombstones. Those are retained evidence, not leaked document content or a failed cleanup.

## Reproduction boundaries

The MySQL checks used `FREEHR_TEST_MYSQL=1`, a newly created disposable database, migrations 0000–0014 and single-worker execution; the database was dropped after the run.

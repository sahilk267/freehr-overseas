# Permission-bound market evidence and account research review

Date: 13 November 2026 (Asia/Calcutta). Partial M02-F04, M09-F02, M10-F01/F02. Reuses canonical intake identities/source access, workflow-event storage, strategy and operating briefs. No complete advanced module is claimed.

## Implemented behavior

- Private owner `/market-evidence` and `marketEvidence.*` APIs manage exact-version internal-use permission declarations against existing **prospect** intake sources. Existing source keys, rotation, disable and workspace locking are reused through one shared authentication helper. There is no second key registry, provider SDK, identity engine, worker or case lifecycle. Candidate source keys cannot acquire market purpose through this API. The new declaration gates the market path; existing candidate/prospect intake contracts are unchanged.
- Declarations record owner-asserted rights basis, contract/provenance reference, public terms URL, start/expiry, allowed observation kinds and a 1–180-day freshness window. Enabled expiry is bounded to the next 366 days. Start may be scheduled; not-started, expired or disabled rights prevent intake/use. Missing rights fail closed. Declaring a contract is not external verification of licensing, facts, hiring authority, agency openness, candidate supply or payment reliability. No external contact, spend or hiring permission is granted.
- `POST /api/integrations/market/:sourceId` accepts an existing source bearer key, a batch key, the current permission revision and up to 50 strictly typed observations. Each row carries an external key/revision, exact canonical employer domain, role-family/region/industry tags, observed timestamp and a public HTTPS citation without credentials/query/fragment/port. Hiring demand stores snapshot counts; salary observations preserve exact integer minor-unit strings, currency, period, range type and sample size; competition and historical seasonality retain explicit source-reported quantities. Candidate/contact fields, claimed verification flags and unknown fields are rejected. Citations are never fetched by this implementation.
- Hostinger Fastify and Express routes use 256 KiB body limits and the same processing command. Source authentication is repeated under the workspace lock after rotation/disable could have changed. Accepted batches require a currently enabled prospect source and a current exact permission revision; emergency stop blocks new commits. The market endpoint permits ten new batches per source/minute; receipt retries consume no new admission. This is its own data-intake quota, not an outbound-contact allowance.
- Accounts must already exist through canonical Intake/CRM. Domain normalization reuses the existing identity function. Missing/ambiguous/unavailable accounts, forbidden kinds, stale/future/out-of-permission observations and future seasonality periods receive held outcomes; their unlinked raw source facts are not persisted. No company, contact or candidate is created/overwritten. A new batch may retry a held record after its prerequisite is corrected; the original receipt stays immutable. HTTP 200 acknowledges a receipt, not successful acceptance of every row.
- Exact batch retries return the original receipt with `receiptOnly` and zero `newlyAccepted`. New batches reuse identical same-revision observations; different content at that revision is a conflict. Corrections require the next revision and prior accepted digest, cannot move observation time backwards and preserve history. Raw external record keys are replaced by hashes in storage. Same-source openings change is derived only for a current, same-scope, later observed snapshot under the same permission revision; same-instant corrections, stale predecessors, changed scope or changed licensing do not become temporal market growth.
- Current use rechecks source availability, exact permission version/window/kind, observed age, canonical account/domain/ambiguity and permanent retirement. Unusable raw fields are masked in current and historical owner APIs. Historical receipts do not make old facts current. A one-second tolerance for database receipt timestamp precision does not permit future source observations. Permanent retirement is owner/version/reason bound, retry safe and terminal for that source external key; receipt or new-batch replay cannot restore it. Stored integrity hashes detect inconsistent read-back, including JSON key ordering, but are not tamper-proof signatures or provider attestations.
- Full bounded source calculation precedes observation filtering/pagination. Current source counts are not distinct vacancy or revenue totals. Exact scope/date groups with different reported opening counts flag **potential** demand conflicts; different roles/regions are not conflated and no winner is invented. Accounts with current reported demand link to governing CRM qualification; agency openness/payment reliability stay unverified. Account verification is the canonical recorded state, not a new external check. Salaries are not converted, pooled or written into actual compensation.
- Strategy shows current source counts and exact industry/region/role-family tag matches separately from owner-entered model probabilities/economics. Source observations do not silently calibrate probability, change fees, allocate exclusive staff or start objectives. Existing daily/weekly briefs receive only minimized counts and source/configuration commitments; raw reports, domains, prices, citation URLs, proof notes and source identifiers are excluded from brief status. Brief snapshot commitments cover this additional context without mixing estimates into invoices/receipts.
- Supported read sets are bounded to 10,000 source/account/relevant-event rows and fail closed instead of emitting partial totals. Observation history exposes the latest 20 versions and receipt history the latest 50, with explicit older-history flags. Account cards display at most 100 while full counts are retained. Physical retention/archival/deletion and enterprise throughput beyond these bounds remain open. Hidden data is not claimed physically erased.

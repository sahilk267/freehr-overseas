# Recruitment strategy, exact economics and capacity proposals

Date: 13 November 2026 (Asia/Calcutta). Partial M09-F01/F03/F05/F06, integrated with M22 operating briefs. No complete advanced module or autonomous agency is claimed.

## Implemented scope

- Private owner `/strategy` and `recruitmentStrategy.*` APIs record versioned niche, industry/region/role-family targets, ideal-client/exclusion criteria, horizon, capacity, gross target, fee assumptions and delivery economics. No strategy is configured by default. Illustrative inputs are unsaved and explicitly hypothetical. Draft, active internal planning and pause modes create no recruiting or spending authority. Reviewed dates use UTC calendar days; active planning rejects stale/future assumptions and fees rounding to zero.
- Exact integer minor-unit calculations separate modeled gross fees, collection-adjusted proceeds, acquisition, sourcing, direct variable, recruiter labor and fixed costs. Percentage fees round once at the currency's minor-unit boundary. Fractional expected placements use conservative millionth-unit precision; costs round upward and proceeds downward. Target placements and contribution break-even are unavailable when the necessary positive fee/unit contribution is missing. No floating-point currency calculation, FX conversion, tax treatment, cash-clearing claim, measured margin or guaranteed earnings is inferred.
- Five deterministic scenarios expose the stated base case, half response, scarce talent (half conversion and 50% extra delivery effort), half mandate demand and halved acquisition/sourcing budgets. Prospect work is capped by both category budget and available minutes; placement volume by modeled conversion, whole-placement delivery capacity and source budget. Fixed, variable and labor costs are additional to the named category budgets. These fixed sensitivity tests are not learned forecasts, live market intelligence or causal evidence.
- Source-bound role assessments reuse canonical jobs/accounts. Owner-declared target fit, candidate supply, feasibility, placement/collection probabilities, fee, delivery effort and direct costs remain independently unverified assertions. Only open roles on available accounts with current assessments, matching currency, positive modeled contribution and exact current strategy/source fingerprints can be allocated. Source fingerprints cover declared role/compensation/requirements/account-state fields; no candidate/contact data or authoritative hiring state is copied into a new record.
- A deterministic greedy proposal ranks eligible roles by exact modeled contribution per delivery minute, then stable source ID. Full-source minute, concurrent-role and sourcing-budget limits apply before display pagination. This is not a globally optimal schedule, verified supply pool, candidate ranking, allocation of exclusive recruiters or an actual revenue forecast. The individual-role scenario and aggregate acquisition scenario are alternatives, not additive projections. Criteria matching remains an explicit owner assessment; no semantic niche or licensed-demand discovery is inferred.
- Current proposals show a daily prospect planning ceiling capped by the current workspace outbound limit. Draft/pause, stale assumptions and emergency stop remove proposed action envelopes. Worker state is visible; no job, lead, outbox, approval, budget reservation, contact permission, actual schedule, invoice or hiring decision is created. All consequential workflows retain their existing gates. Planning workdays are declared units, not dated recipient availability or an actual per-day task distribution.
- Settings and role commands use the existing workspace lock, expected revisions and exact retry identities. Changed strategy or relevant job/account facts invalidate assessments; corrections append versions. Stored financial/authority and role-assumption hashes detect inconsistent read-back, including JSON key reordering; they are not tamper-proof signatures. Owner history exposes the latest 20 assumption versions with an older-history flag and does not reconstruct historical outcomes from current sources. Every configured source/history set is bounded; >10,000 rows fails closed rather than returning a truncated allocation.
- Existing daily/weekly operating briefs include only configuration revision, mode, freshness and evidence level. Private strategic notes, target-client criteria, role assessment identities and hypothetical revenues are excluded from brief status and canonical financial totals. No new schema, credential, provider, LLM, timer, workflow engine or notification route is introduced.

# Versioned commercial agreements and contract-derived placement obligations

Date: 11 November 2026 (Asia/Calcutta). Target: `https://freelancehr.overseasjob.in`.

## Connected implementation

The existing feeProposals record, role feeProposalId and shared workflow ledger now support source-versioned commercial terms, exact terms hashes, immutable history, optimistic draft edits, exact create retries, owner-evidenced acceptance and unaccepted draft withdrawal. Accepted terms cannot be edited. An amendment is a separate proposal and retains its parent reference; competing active amendment forks are denied. A newer accepted amendment blocks using its parent for new role bindings while prior bound placements retain their original obligation. Owner-recorded acceptance remains explicitly unverified client identity/signature; conversion is a CRM stage and does not establish external commercial acceptance.

An owned draft role can opt into an exact accepted proposal receipt before any placement exists. A pending offer then freezes explicit annual compensation in currency minor units, the accepted terms and the original placement source fingerprint. Percentage/fixed fees and explicit tax rates use integer arithmetic and half-up minor-unit rounding, without FX assumptions. Compensation and currency mutations invalidate the commercial context. Existing offer, placement confirmation, manual invoice draft and invoice issuance entry points repeat obligation checks so a parallel status path cannot override the bound terms. Candidate suppression is checked before recording new compensation and confirming a new bound joining; erasure redacts new compensation snapshots and prevents late joining resurrection.

Bound joining confirmations use their contract guarantee duration, including zero days. Legacy unbound workflows retain their prior default; historical compensation units are not silently reinterpreted. Accepted contract terms do not themselves authenticate a joining or offer. UI distinguishes explicit contract compensation from unit-unverified legacy amounts. Existing record/state and mandatory owner-decision controls remain in force.

Contract invoice drafts derive exact fees/tax/currency, reserve one atomic workspace/year sequence number, and retain immutable agreement source evidence. One current placement obligation cannot fork active invoices or re-bill settled/credited/written-off/closed obligations. Unused cancelled drafts remain historical; a fresh unused replacement consumes its own number. Contract payment terms derive dueAt from actual issuance through the existing approval engine, not the draft date. Manually entered invoice overrides are rejected on bound placements. Cancelling an unissued draft requires its current fingerprint, refuses issued/payment-bearing obligations and atomically revokes unused issue approvals/timers; no payment or receipt is fabricated. Monetary values are bounded to existing signed-INT storage limits and no FX/tax law assumptions are inferred.

Owner controls are integrated into Prospects, Placements and Finance. No new scheduler, sender, financial engine, user table or SQL migration is introduced. The existing generated invoice document and approval/collection paths are reused.

## Limits and remaining roadmap

The module registry/backlog/remaining register reflect partial package implementation. Legacy unbound roles/placements are not silently migrated. Existing active-role amendments, compensation amendments after offer, retainers/milestones, jurisdiction-specific tax/numbering/legal rules, authenticated e-sign/KYB and full replacement/refund obligations remain open. The new signature/acceptance source is an owner statement, not an external provider receipt. Payment provider checkout/webhooks/clearing and genuine economic outcomes remain separate open gates. Payment/guarantee days currently mean elapsed 24-hour days; business-day/holiday rules need explicit configuration. No whole advanced module or complete autonomous revenue journey is claimed.
